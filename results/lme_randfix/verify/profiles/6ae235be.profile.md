### Refining Process Descriptions
- Assistant (2023-05-22): Atmospheric distillation is the first step in refining, where crude oil is heated and separated into different fractions based on boiling points.
- Assistant (2023-05-22): Fluid catalytic cracking (FCC) breaks down heavier fractions of crude oil into lighter, more valuable products such as gasoline and diesel fuel.
- Assistant (2023-05-22): Alkylation combines smaller molecules to form larger ones, creating high-octane gasoline components.
- Assistant (2023-05-22): Hydrotreating removes sulfur and other impurities from gasoline and diesel fractions to improve their quality.
- Assistant (2023-05-22): Delayed coking breaks down heavy, residual oils into lighter products such as naphtha, diesel, and petroleum coke.
- Assistant (2023-05-22): Hydrocracking uses hydrogen and a catalyst to break down heavier fractions of crude oil into lighter, more valuable products.
