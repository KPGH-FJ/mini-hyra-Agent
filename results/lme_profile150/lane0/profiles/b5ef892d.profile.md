### White Rim Road (assistant noted)
- White Rim Road is a 100-mile dirt road down to the White Rim Plateau, suitable for high-clearance vehicles, requiring a permit.
- A High-Clearance Vehicle Permit is required for all vehicles; obtainable at the Canyonlands Visitor Center or online.
- A Backcountry Permit with a fee is required for overnight camping or backcountry activities.
- Requires a high-clearance 4x4 vehicle with at least 8–10 inches of ground clearance and tires in good condition.
- Canyonlands limits groups on the White Rim Road to 10 vehicles.
- A typical White Rim Road trip takes 2–3 days depending on pace and activities.
- Suggested itinerary: Day 1 Island in the Sky to Murphy Hogback Campground; Day 2 to White Crack Campground; Day 3 return to Island in the Sky.
- There are no services or fuel stations along the White Rim Road; bring enough fuel, food, and water.
