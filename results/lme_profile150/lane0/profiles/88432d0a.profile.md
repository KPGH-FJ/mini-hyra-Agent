Assistant strawberry tart tips (2023-05-23):
- Use fresh strawberries that are sweet and slightly tart for the best flavor and texture
- Limit added sugar; a light honey drizzle suffices
- Add a squeeze of fresh lemon or orange juice to enhance strawberry flavor and brightness
- Use a flaky crust (pâte brisée or high-butter pastry dough) for textural contrast
- Top with fresh whipped cream or crème fraîche to balance sweetness
- Arrangement ideas: simple slice pattern, fresh flower pattern, alternating pattern, random scatter, or strawberry crown

Assistant tart crust flour advice (2023-05-23):
- All-purpose flour: classic choice balancing strength, tenderness, and flavor
- Pastry flour (low protein): tender, flaky, delicate crust
- Cake flour (very low protein): tender flaky crusts, though harder to work with
- Caution: whole wheat flour's nutty flavor may overpower strawberries and make a denser, less flaky crust

Assistant wing advice (2023-05-23):
- Dry brine: pat dry, salt both sides, refrigerate at least 2 hours or overnight
- Coat with 1–2 tbsp baking powder plus 1 tbsp salt to crisp the skin
- Bake at 425°F (220°C) using the convection setting
- Bake 30–35 minutes until cooked through
- Broil 5–7 minutes until the skin is crispy and golden brown
- Raise oven to 450°F (230°C) for the last 10–15 minutes for extra crispiness
