### Dessert mango salsa tips (@2023-06-16)
- Make the dessert mango salsa sweeter and less spicy than the jerk chicken salsa.
- Increase mango (ripe plus slightly underripe) for a sweeter, fruit-forward salsa.
- Adjust lime juice to balance sweetness and cut the richness of the coconut panna cotta.
- Use honey, agave nectar, or brown sugar to balance the salsa's flavors.
- Add fresh mint, basil, or cilantro for a bright, refreshing salsa flavor.
- Optional splash of Grand Marnier or orange-flavored liqueur.

### Dessert mango salsa recipe (@2023-06-16)
- 2 ripe mangos, diced; 1/2 red onion, diced; 1/4 cup fresh cilantro, chopped; 2 tablespoons lime juice; 1 tablespoon honey or agave nectar (optional); salt to taste.
