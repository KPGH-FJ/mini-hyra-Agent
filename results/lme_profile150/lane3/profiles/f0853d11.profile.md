## Assistant-Provided Reference Knowledge

### Sunscreen chemical science
- (assistant noted) Oxybenzone, a benzophenone-type chemical, causes coral bleaching, damages coral DNA, and harms sea urchins, fish, and mussels
- (assistant noted) Octinoxate causes coral bleaching, induces coral larvae to encyst preventing new colonies, and affects sea anemone development and growth
- (assistant noted) Sunscreen chemicals wash off swimmers' skin into the water, especially in high-tourist areas, and accumulate in water and sediment, exacerbating threats to coral reefs

### Making Strides Against Breast Cancer (ACS)
- (assistant noted) Founded in 1988 in Boston; first event had 200 participants and raised $30,000
- (assistant noted) Has grown to over 1 million participants in more than 200 US locations
- (assistant noted) Format: 5K walk/run held in July and August, with some locations hosting events in October
- (assistant noted) Has raised over $875 million; ACS has contributed to a 39% decline in US breast cancer deaths since 1990

### PurpleStride (Pancreatic Cancer Action Network)
- (assistant noted) Founded in 2008; first event held in San Diego; now over 60 locations
- (assistant noted) Format: 5K walk/run held in June, July, and August, with some locations in September and October; includes a timed 5K run, 5K walk, kids' dash, and virtual option
- (assistant noted) Has raised over $100 million, funding over 150 research grants and supporting over 100,000 patients and families
