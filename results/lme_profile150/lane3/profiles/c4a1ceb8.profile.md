### Assistant-provided honey syrup guidance
- Assistant advised honey syrup is a 1:1 ratio of honey dissolved in water, usable as a substitute for simple syrup in many cocktails (2023-05-21)
- Assistant advised honey syrup adds rich complex flavor with floral, fruity, nutty notes; honey is sweeter than sugar so less is needed; honey syrup is thicker and more viscous than simple syrup (2023-05-21)
- Assistant advised homemade simple syrup lasts 2-3 months refrigerated; commercial or 2:1 high-sugar simple syrup can last 6 months or longer (2023-05-21)
- Assistant advised honey syrup lasts 2-4 weeks refrigerated; with high-quality low-water honey like manuka or buckwheat it may last 2-3 months (2023-05-21)
- Assistant suggested infusing honey by combining cleaned dried ingredients in a glass jar, covering with honey, sealing and steeping in a cool dark place 1-4 weeks, shaking every day or two, then straining through cheesecloth or fine sieve (2023-05-21)
- Assistant suggested lavender honey syrup: 1 cup dried lavender buds and 2 cups honey, steep 2-3 weeks shaking every day or two, strain, store in airtight container in refrigerator (2023-05-21)
- Assistant suggested rose-petal honey syrup: 1 cup dried rose petals and 2 cups honey, steep 1-2 weeks shaking every day or two, strain, store in airtight container in refrigerator (2023-05-21)
- Assistant suggested citrus and spice pairings: orange and cinnamon, lemon and ginger, grapefruit and cardamom, lime and nutmeg (2023-05-21)
- Assistant suggested citrus and spice honey syrup: 1 cup honey, 1/2 cup citrus peel, 1/4 cup spices steeped 2-4 weeks, strained, then 1/4 cup citrus juice stirred in; store refrigerated in airtight container (2023-05-21)
- Assistant advised honey infusion strength by time: 1-2 weeks for light subtle flavor, 2-4 weeks for balanced flavor, 4-6 weeks for strong intense flavor (2023-05-21)
- Assistant suggested straining infused syrup by pouring through a fine-mesh sieve or cheesecloth set over a clean bowl, discarding solids; optionally filter through coffee filter or paper towel for clearer syrup (2023-05-21)
