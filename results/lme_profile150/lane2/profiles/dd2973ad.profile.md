### Assistant-suggested drinks (2023-05-25)

- Infused Water: add slices of lemon, lime, cucumber, or berries to a pitcher of water
- Herbal tea: peppermint, chamomile, or hibiscus — caffeine-free and rich in antioxidants
- Green tea: low-caffeine, rich in antioxidants, may help boost metabolism

- Low-sugar sports drinks like Nuun or ZYM to replenish electrolytes after high-intensity exercise
- Kombucha: fermented tea with probiotics that may aid digestion and boost the immune system
- Vegetable juice: carrots, beets, cucumbers; limit fruit juices due to high sugar content
- Almond milk or other unsweetened, unflavored non-dairy milks for tea, coffee, or smoothie bases
- Cucumber Lime Refresher: diced cucumber, fresh lime juice, and mint leaves in a pitcher of water

### Assistant infused water tips (2023-05-25)
- Slice lemon and cucumber thinly and add to a pitcher of water
- Let infused water sit in the refrigerator at least 30 minutes so flavors meld
- Use a clean, sanitized pitcher and utensils to avoid contaminating the water
- Change the water and add fresh slices every 24 hours to prevent bacterial growth
- Experiment with different combinations of fruits, herbs, and spices for unique flavors
