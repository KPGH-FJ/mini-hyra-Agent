- Amazon Swords lighting: tolerate low to high lighting; moderate to high lighting (2–4 watts per gallon) yields vibrant colors and faster growth (2023-05-23).
- Amazon Swords water params: prefer soft to moderately hard water (5–15 dGH), pH 6.5–8.5, temperature 72–82°F (22–28°C) (2023-05-23).
- Amazon Swords substrate: need a nutrient-rich substrate 1–2 inches (2.5–5 cm) deep (2023-05-23).
- Amazon Swords fertilization: heavy feeders that benefit from regular fertilization with a balanced fertilizer (e.g., 10-10-10 NPK) (2023-05-23).
- Amazon Swords pruning: can grow up to 20 inches (50 cm); trimming the top inch or two encourages bushy growth (2023-05-23).
- Amazon Swords propagation: by dividing the rhizome or by planting the seeds they produce (2023-05-23).
- Amazon Swords temperature: ideal 72–82°F (22–28°C), tolerable range 68–86°F (20–30°C) (2023-05-23).
- Water Wisteria lighting: prefers moderate to high lighting (2–4 watts per gallon) but tolerates low lighting with fewer leaves and slower growth (2023-05-23).
- Water Wisteria water params: prefers soft to moderately hard water (5–15 dGH), pH 6.5–8.5, temperature 72–82°F (22–28°C) (2023-05-23).
- Water Wisteria substrate: prefers a deeper substrate of 2–3 inches (5–7.5 cm) with a nutrient-rich composition (2023-05-23).
- Water Wisteria fertilization: fast-growing, benefits from regular fertilization with a balanced fertilizer (e.g., 10-10-10 NPK) (2023-05-23).
- Water Wisteria pruning: can grow up to 30 inches (75 cm); trimming the longest stems encourages new growth (2023-05-23).
- Water Wisteria propagation: by cutting 2–3 inch (5–7.5 cm) stem tips and planting them, by dividing roots, or via runners (2023-05-23).
- Water Wisteria temperature: ideal 75–85°F (24–29°C), tolerable range 70–90°F (21–32°C) (2023-05-23).
- Assistant plant care tips: both Amazon Swords and Water Wisteria need adequate water circulation and oxygenation to avoid fungal infections in stagnant water; over-handling can cause stress, disease, or algae growth; both grow large so placement should be adjusted over time to leave enough space (2023-05-23).
- Assistant temperature tips: sudden temperature changes are more stressful for aquatic plants than gradual changes; tank temperature should be kept consistent with minimal daily fluctuations; temperature extremes can come from direct sunlight, heaters, or cold water changes; temperature affects the tank's fish and other organisms, not just the plants (2023-05-23).
- Assistant heater advice: a submersible or hang-on-back aquarium heater can maintain ideal plant temperature, sized to the tank (2023-05-23).

### Fertilizer / Iron
- Assistant assessed: API CO2 Booster mainly provides a CO2 boost with only some micronutrients, so it is not a comprehensive fertilizer for aquatic plants (2023-05-23).
- Assistant recommended Seachem Flourish as a balanced fertilizer providing nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, and micronutrients (2023-05-23).
- Assistant recommended API Aquarium Fertilizer as a balanced fertilizer providing nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, and micronutrients (2023-05-23).
- Assistant recommended Tropica Plant Nutrition as a premium, high-quality balanced fertilizer with a broad nutrient spectrum (2023-05-23).
- Assistant suggested: for Amazon Swords and Water Wisteria, start with 1–2 teaspoons of fertilizer per 10 gallons of water, 1–2 times a week, then adjust; start at a low dose and gradually increase based on plant growth and water test results (2023-05-23).
- Assistant explained micronutrient functions: micronutrients like iron, copper, and manganese support photosynthesis by aiding the electron transport chain; iron is a key component of chlorophyll; zinc, copper, and manganese act as enzyme cofactors; calcium and boron are involved in cell wall formation and maintenance (2023-05-23).
- Assistant explained iron deficiency symptoms: chlorosis (yellowing or whitening of leaves) because chlorophyll production is impaired; stunted growth with weak, spindly stems and reduced leaf production; iron-deficient plants are more prone to disease and pests (2023-05-23).
- Assistant suggested: use a fertilizer containing chelated iron (e.g., iron gluconate or iron EDTA), which aquatic plants absorb more easily; good water circulation prevents iron-poor areas; adequate lighting matters for iron use; start iron dosing low to avoid overdosing since excessive iron can cause toxicity (2023-05-23).
- Assistant suggested Seachem Iron, API Iron Supplement, and Tropica Iron Fertilizer as iron-rich fertilizers for Amazon Swords and Water Wisteria (2023-05-23).
