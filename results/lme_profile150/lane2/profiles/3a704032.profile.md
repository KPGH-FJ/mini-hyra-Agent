## Hobbies & Interests — Plant Care
### Peace lily
- User read that a peace lily losing some leaves is normal (2023-05-21).
- (Assistant suggested) Losing a few leaves is normal while a peace lily adjusts to a new environment (2023-05-21).
- (Assistant suggested) Peace lilies prefer bright, indirect light; direct sun can scorch leaves; east- or west-facing windows are ideal (2023-05-21).
- (Assistant suggested) Overwatering is the most common cause of death in peace lilies; water only when soil is dry to the first knuckle and ensure good drainage (2023-05-21).
- (Assistant suggested) Water when the top 1–2 inches of soil feel dry: roughly every 7–10 days in spring/summer and every 10–14 days in fall/winter (2023-05-21).
- (Assistant suggested) Peace lilies prefer humid conditions but adapt to average humidity; use a pebble tray or humidifier (2023-05-21).
- (Assistant suggested) Mist peace lilies 1–2 times per week in average conditions, or every 3–4 days in dry environments; mist in the morning with room-temperature water, avoiding the crown (2023-05-21).
- (Assistant suggested) Keep peace lilies at a consistent 65–80°F (18–27°C), away from vents, fireplaces, and drafty windows; ideal 68–72°F (20–22°C), acceptable 65–75°F (18–24°C); the user's 65–70°F range is fine; avoid below 55°F or above 85°F (2023-05-21).
- (Assistant suggested) Feed with balanced water-soluble fertilizer (20-20-20) during the growing season, diluted to half the recommended strength (2023-05-21).
- (Assistant suggested) Peace lilies prefer a balanced fertilizer with slightly higher nitrogen (e.g., 20-15-15 or 20-20-20); high-phosphorus bloom boosters can weaken foliage growth (2023-05-21).
- (Assistant suggested) Fertilize every 1–2 weeks during the growing season (spring/summer) and once a month during dormancy (fall/winter) (2023-05-21).
- (Assistant suggested) Use a well-draining potting mix; tropical or orchid-specific mixes are good choices (2023-05-21).
- (Assistant suggested) Remove dead or dying leaves and flowers with clean, sharp scissors to prevent disease spread (2023-05-21).
- (Assistant suggested) Repot in spring or summer if outgrown, using a slightly larger pot and fresh potting mix (2023-05-21).
- (Assistant suggested) Check regularly for spider mites, mealybugs, or scale; treat with insecticidal soap or neem oil (2023-05-21).
- (Assistant suggested) Summary care: bright indirect light, watering when the top inch of soil is dry, humidity above 50%, temperatures of 65–80°F (2023-05-21).

### Fern
- (Assistant suggested) Ferns thrive in very humid conditions, often above 50% relative humidity, while peace lilies tolerate around 40–50% (2023-05-21).
- (Assistant suggested) Mist the fern 2–3 times per week with distilled or filtered water in the morning; ferns prefer 40–60% relative humidity; avoid getting water on the crown (2023-05-23).
- (Assistant suggested) The tiny moving dots on the fern are likely spider mites or springtails; spider mites feed on sap and may leave webbing; springtails are harmless to plants (2023-05-23).
- (Assistant suggested) Organic pest control for ferns: neem oil, soap solution, insecticidal soap, horticultural oil; also isolate the plant, prune infested areas, improve air circulation (2023-05-23).
- (Assistant suggested) Suggested approach: treat with neem oil, wait a few days, then mist 2–3 times a week to prevent pests returning (2023-05-23).

### Basil
- (Assistant suggested) Basil thrives in partial shade; move to a spot with indirect sunlight or use a sheer curtain/shade cloth (2023-05-23).
- (Assistant suggested) Basil in shadier spots needs less water; check soil moisture to the first knuckle and water only when dry (2023-05-23).
- (Assistant suggested) Basil loves humid environments; use a tray of water and pebbles or a humidifier to maintain humidity (2023-05-23).
- (Assistant suggested) Feed basil with balanced water-soluble fertilizer at half the recommended strength; overfertilization is detrimental (2023-05-23).
- (Assistant suggested) Pinch off basil flower buds to encourage leaf growth; trim leggy stems to keep a bushy shape (2023-05-23).
- (Assistant suggested) Basil prefers daytime temperatures between 65–75°F (18–24°C); avoid drafty areas and extreme temperature fluctuations (2023-05-23).
- (Assistant suggested) Basil needs well-draining soil with perlite, vermiculite, or sand to prevent waterlogging and root rot (2023-05-23).
- (Assistant suggested) Basil prefers slightly acidic to neutral soil pH ranging from 6.0 to 7.0 (2023-05-23).
- (Assistant suggested) Basil loves soil rich in organic matter such as compost or peat moss for moisture, temperature regulation, and nutrients (2023-05-23).
- (Assistant suggested) DIY basil potting mix: 2 parts peat moss or coconut coir, 1 part perlite or vermiculite, 1 part compost or worm castings, 1 part general-purpose potting soil (2023-05-23).
