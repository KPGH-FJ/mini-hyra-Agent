### Assistant suggestions — recipes & ingredients
- Misir wot recipe yields 4–6 servings (2023-05-21); uses red lentils, onions, garlic, ginger, cumin, coriander, turmeric, paprika, cayenne, diced tomatoes, vegetable broth, and niter kibbeh or oil; simmers covered 20–25 minutes until lentils are tender; traditionally served with injera bread, or with rice, naan, or crusty bread (2023-05-21).
- Injera recipe yields 4–6 injera (2023-05-21); uses teff flour, all-purpose flour, active dry yeast, water, salt, and optional fenugreek seeds; sourdough starter and dough each ferment 24–48 hours in a warm place; homemade injera can be stored airtight up to 3 days or frozen up to 2 months; if teff flour is unavailable, substitute buckwheat flour or a mix of whole wheat and all-purpose flour (2023-05-21).
- Injera sourdough starter: mix 1/2 cup teff flour with 1/2 cup water and ferment 24–48 hours at 75–80°F; starter is ready when bubbly, expanded, frothy, and sour-smelling, after 2–3 days of feeding; feed daily with 1/2 cup teff flour and 1/2 cup water, refrigerated starters need weekly feeding (2023-05-21).
- Teff flour is gluten-free, high in protein, rich in fiber and iron, with a dark color and nutty flavor; teff is an ancient grain native to Ethiopia and Eritrea, staple in their cuisines; teff absorbs more liquid than other flours and can be used 1:1 with adjustments; store airtight in a cool dry place (2023-05-21).
- Naan recipe: 1 1/2 cups atta flour, 1/4 tsp salt, 1/4 tsp sugar, 1/2 tsp yeast, 1/2 cup lukewarm water, 2 tbsp ghee; yields 6–8 portions, each cooked 1–2 minutes until puffed and blistered (2023-05-22).
- Assistant naan tips: use atta (chapati) flour, all-purpose flour can be substituted with slightly different results; dough should rest at least 1–2 hours or overnight in the refrigerator to develop gluten; cook at 500°F or as hot as the oven goes, on a hot skillet or preheated baking stone; don't overwork the dough or the bread will be dense and tough; brush with melted butter or ghee for rich flavor and golden color (2023-05-22).
- Saffron-infused simple syrup recipe: 1 cup sugar, 1 cup water, 1/2 teaspoon saffron threads (10–12 threads); saffron threads should be soaked in 1 tablespoon hot water for 5–10 minutes before adding; syrup should simmer 5–7 minutes until slightly thickened, then be strained and cooled; can be stored in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks (2023-05-22).
- Saffron Gin Fizz recipe: 1 1/2 oz gin, 1/2 oz saffron syrup, 1/2 oz lemon juice, splash of soda water (2023-05-22).
- Saffron batch quantities: 2x syrup batch (2 cups) needs 1–1.25 teaspoons saffron threads (20–25 threads); 3x batch (3 cups) needs 1.5–1.75 teaspoons (30–35 threads); 4x batch (4 cups) needs 2–2.25 teaspoons (40–45 threads) (2023-05-22).
- Saffron storage: airtight container in a dark, cool, dry place at 60–70°F; high-quality threads retain flavor and aroma 2–3 years when stored properly; medium-quality lasts 1–2 years; low-quality lasts a few months to a year; frozen threads can last up to 5 years at 0°F or below (2023-05-22).
- High-quality saffron regions: La Mancha (Spain), Kashmir (India), and Iran; high-quality saffron ranges from $5 to $15 per gram depending on quality and origin (2023-05-22).
- Assistant recommended saffron sources: Penzeys Spices, The Spice House, Amazon, Middle Eastern/Indian grocers, specialty food stores (2023-05-22).
- Vegan stir-fry protein alternatives suggested by assistant: seitan, edamame, lentils, mushrooms, jackfruit, chickpeas, vegan meat alternatives (Beyond Meat, Impossible Foods, Gardein), and nuts/seeds (2023-05-24).
- Seitan facts from assistant: made from wheat gluten, chewy meat-like texture, 20–30 grams of protein per 3-ounce serving (2023-05-24).
- Tempeh facts from assistant: fermented soybean product from Indonesia, nutty flavor, 15–18 grams of protein per 3-ounce serving, rich in probiotics (2023-05-24).
- Cashew mozzarella recipe from assistant: 1 cup cashews, 1/2 cup water, 1 tbsp lemon juice, 1/2 tsp salt, 1/4 tsp nutritional yeast, 1/4 tsp garlic powder; chill at least 4 hours (2023-05-24).
- Cashew feta recipe from assistant: 1 cup cashews, 1/2 cup water, 1 tbsp lemon juice, 1/2 tsp salt, 1/4 tsp nutritional yeast, 1/4 tsp dried oregano, 1/4 tsp garlic powder; crumble and chill (2023-05-24).
- Cashew parmesan recipe from assistant: 1 cup cashews, 1/4 cup nutritional yeast, 1 tbsp lemon juice, 1/2 tsp salt, 1/4 tsp garlic powder; shape into block and chill (2023-05-24).
- Cashew brie recipe from assistant: 1 cup cashews, 1/2 cup water, 1 tbsp lemon juice, 1/2 tsp salt, 1/4 tsp nutritional yeast, 1/4 tsp garlic powder, 1/4 tsp dried thyme; shape into wheel and chill (2023-05-24).
- Assistant cashew cheese tips: soak cashews before blending, use lemon juice, garlic, salt and nutritional yeast for flavor, and thickeners like tapioca starch or arrowroot powder (2023-05-24).
- Assistant recommended fermented food pairings for kimchi: miso (fermented soybean paste), sourdough bread, sauerkraut, kefir (fermented milk drink packed with probiotics), fermented cucumbers/pickles, natto (fermented soybean dish packed with protein), kombucha (fermented tea drink packed with probiotics) (2023-05-25).
- Basic kimchi recipe from assistant: 2 lbs napa cabbage, coarse salt, gochugaru, garlic, ginger, fish sauce, rice vinegar, water; ferment at room temperature 1–5 days (2023-05-25).
- Assistant noted seafood like shrimp or anchovies adds a salty, umami flavor to kimchi; garlic scapes add a pungent, aromatic flavor (2023-05-25).
