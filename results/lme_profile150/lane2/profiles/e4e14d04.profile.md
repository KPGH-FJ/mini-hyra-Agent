### Plot & character background (assistant provided, 2023-05-24)
- *The Lincoln Lawyer*: defense attorney Mickey Haller works from his Lincoln Town Car and defends wealthy real estate agent Louis Roulet, uncovering deceit and corruption.
- Mickey Haller described as a smooth-talking, street-smart defense attorney and hustler with a conscience, wit, and personal demons.

### Mickey Haller series reading order (assistant provided, 2023-05-24)
- *The Lincoln Lawyer* (2005) — first Mickey Haller book, introducing the defense attorney who works from his Lincoln Town Car.
- *The Brass Verdict* (2008) — Mickey inherits a lucrative practice from a murdered colleague and lands in a killer's crosshairs.
- *The Drop* (2011) — Mickey takes on a high-profile murder case involving a former client while dealing with personal demons.
- *The Gods of Guilt* (2013) — Mickey defends a former client accused of murder and uncovers a web of deceit.
- *The Crossing* (2015) — Mickey teams up with Detective Harry Bosch on a case involving a former client and a complex conspiracy.
- *The Law of Innocence* (2020) — Mickey is accused of murder and must clear his name.
- Assistant advised the Mickey Haller books can be read standalone but are best read in order; suggested starting the Harry Bosch series with *The Poet* (1996) before crossing over.
