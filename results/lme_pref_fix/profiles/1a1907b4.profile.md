## Assistant-Suggested Facts

### Cocktail Recipes (assistant suggested, @2023-05-22)
- **Pimm's Cup with a Twist:** 1.5 oz Hendrick's gin, 0.5 oz lemon juice, 0.5 oz simple syrup, 0.5 oz Pimm's No. 1, splash of ginger beer.
- **Cucumber Gimlet:** 2 oz Hendrick's gin, 1 oz lime juice, 0.5 oz simple syrup, 0.5 oz cucumber juice.
- **Rose Petal Spritz:** 2 oz Hendrick's gin, 2 oz Prosecco, 1 oz rose syrup, 1 oz lemon-lime soda.
- **Gin-Gin Mule:** 2 oz Hendrick's gin, 4 oz ginger beer, squeeze of lime juice.
- **Summer Solstice:** 2 oz Hendrick's gin, 1 oz peach puree, 1 oz lemon juice, 0.5 oz honey syrup.
- **Hendrick's High Tea:** 1 oz steeped tea, 2 oz Hendrick's gin, 1 oz lemon juice, 0.5 oz honey syrup.

### Simple Syrup Recipes & Storage (assistant suggested, @2023-05-22)
- Classic simple syrup: 1 cup sugar dissolved in 1 cup water, heated and cooled.
- Simple syrup can be stored in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks.
- Cucumber simple syrup: add sliced cucumbers while cooling, infuse at least 30 minutes.
- Mint simple syrup: add fresh mint sprigs while cooling for a minty twist.
- Lemon simple syrup: add a slice of lemon while cooling for a brighter citrusy flavor.

### Citrus Syrup Recommendations (assistant suggested, @2023-05-22)
- Grapefruit simple syrup: bitterness balances Pimm's sweetness, citrus enhances Hendrick's botanicals.
- Lemon-ginger simple syrup: adds warmth and spice, ginger complements Hendrick's spicy notes.
- Orange-blossom simple syrup: floral fragrance complements rose and cucumber notes in Hendrick's.

### Grapefruit Varieties (assistant provided, @2023-05-22)
- Ruby Red Grapefruit: deep red color, sweet-tart flavor, gives syrup a pink hue and balanced flavor.
- White Grapefruit ("White Lady"): pale yellow skin, tart and slightly sweet, more pronounced bitterness.
- Pink Grapefruit: sweet tangy flavor and pink color, yields a sweeter, more flavorful syrup.
