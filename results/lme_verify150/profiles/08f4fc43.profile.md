### Charity / organization recommendations
- Assistant recommended Catholic Charities as a network providing food, shelter, and housing assistance to vulnerable populations (@2023-02-20)
  - Services: housing/shelter, food and nutrition, family and children's, senior, immigration and refugee, disaster response, health and wellness, and education and employment services
  - Housing options: emergency shelters, transitional housing (up to 24 months), permanent supportive housing, and rapid re-housing
  - Housing support services include case management, job training/education, counseling and mental health, and on-site health services
  - Path to independence: crisis intervention, assessment/goal-setting, skill-building, housing placement, and follow-up aftercare
  - Training programs: vocational training, soft skills training, computer literacy, entrepreneurship, and industry-specific training
  - Education: GED preparation, English as a Second Language (ESL) classes, and college prep services
  - Employment support: job placement, resume building, job coaching, and networking opportunities
  - Microloans typically range from $1,000 to $10,000 with repayment terms of 6–24 months
  - Grants typically range from $1,000 to $5,000
  - Microloan application requires a business plan, financial projections, credit history, and program participation; poor credit is not necessarily disqualifying
  - Grant application requires a business plan, financial projections, and intended use of funds; some grants target women- or minority-owned businesses
  - Entrepreneurship program has launched food-based, service-based, creative, home-based, and retail businesses
  - Entrepreneurship support includes business planning, training and mentorship, access to capital, networking, and ongoing support
  - Catholic Charities USA website (catholiccharitiesusa.org) has a 'Find a Local Agency' tool to locate the nearest office; local diocese/parish can also help
  - Ways to get involved: volunteering, donating, advocacy, internships, and employment
