- Assistant suggested presentation tips (2023-01-13): define purpose and objectives, use the Simple-Clear-Concise framework, organize logically, use visual aids, highlight key takeaways, use storytelling, encourage interaction, provide context, use positive language, and practice delivery.

### Active Listening & Conflict Resolution
- Assistant recommended active listening practices (2023-01-13): give undivided attention, use verbal/non-verbal cues, paraphrase and summarize, ask open-ended questions, avoid interrupting, listen without judgment, show empathy, take notes, practice mindfulness, and follow up on commitments.
- Assistant advised on active listening (2023-01-13): give undivided attention, use verbal/non-verbal cues, paraphrase and summarize, avoid interrupting, and practice empathy.
- Assistant recommended active listening resources (2023-01-13): "The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People" by Stephen Covey, MindTools' "Active Listening: The Art of Empathetic Conversation", and HBR's "The Power of Active Listening".
- Assistant advised on conflict resolution (2023-01-13): stay calm, focus on the issue not the person, use "I" statements, seek common ground, and be open to compromise.
- Assistant recommended conflict resolution resources (2023-01-13): "Crucial Conversations" by Kerry Patterson, Joseph Grenny, Ron McMillan, and Al Switzler; MindTools' dispute resolution guide; Coursera's "How to Resolve Conflicts at Work".

### Meeting Preparation & Agenda
- Assistant advised (2023-01-13) preparing a clear agenda, encouraging open communication, taking notes, and sending a follow-up summary with action items after the meeting.
- Assistant suggested an agenda template (2023-01-13) with columns Topic, Priority, Time Allocation, Objective; example rows: Project Update (High, 15 min), New Feature Discussion (Medium, 30 min), Team Member Feedback (Low, 10 min).
- Assistant suggested designating a "parking lot" (2023-01-13) for non-essential topics to keep the meeting focused on high-priority items.
- Assistant suggested handling important off-agenda topics (2023-01-13) by: adding to the parking lot, scheduling a separate discussion, giving a brief update, or delegating to a smaller group.
