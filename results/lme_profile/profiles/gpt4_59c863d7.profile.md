## Assistant Suggestions & Advice

### Weathering techniques (assistant suggested, 2023-05-21)
- Washes for creating shadows in recessed areas.
- Drybrushing for textured, worn looks on raised surfaces.
- Pigments for subtle grime and dirt effects.
- Chipping for a battle-worn look.
- Fading for sun-bleached effects.
- Salt weathering for rust and corrosion effects.
- Oils and enamels for blended weathering effects.
- Rust and corrosion products for realistic weathering.
- Mud and dirt effects using pigments and binding agents.
- Exhaust stains using dark washes or oil paint.
- Experimenting with salt weathering and hairspray weathering techniques to add realism.

### Weathering products (assistant suggested, 2023-05-21)
- AK Interactive weathering products for scale models.
- Vallejo weathering products for scale models.
- Tamiya Weathering Master products.
- Mig Productions weathering products.
- Enamel washes compatible with acrylic paints: Humbrol, Testors, Mig Productions, and AK Interactive.

### Enamel wash application (assistant advised, 2023-05-21)
- Thin enamel washes with enamel thinner or mineral spirits, test on a small area first, and build up layers gradually.

### Paints, primers, thinners & finishes (assistant suggested, 2023-05-21)
- Metal-specific primers: Alclad II and Vallejo Metal Primer.
- Metallic paints for a realistic finish: Alclad, Vallejo Metal Color, and Model Master Metalizers.
- Metal polishes: Brasso or Simichrome, used after sanding up to 4000 or 6000 grit.
- Thinners: Tamiya acrylic thinner for Alclad and Vallejo paints, and Vallejo Thinner for Vallejo paints.
- Wet palettes: Vallejo Wet Palette or Citadel Palette.
- Metallic varnishes to enhance sheen: Alclad Aqua Gloss and Vallejo Metal Varnish.
- Alclad Micro Metallics — fine powdered metal particles in a carrier solution — for a subtle metallic sheen.
- Smaller airbrush nozzle (0.2–0.5 mm) is ideal for fine details; larger nozzle (0.5–1.0 mm) suits basecoating.

### PE adhesives & soldering (assistant suggested, 2023-05-29)
- Alternatives to super glue for PE parts: white glue (PVA), Gator's Grip, Tacky Wax, Micro-Mark Micro-Set, two-part epoxy.
- Vallejo acrylics adhere well to photo-etched parts; light priming with metal or Vallejo primer advised.
- Use low-temperature solder melting around 180–200 °F (82–93 °C) for model building.

### '69 Camaro wire detailing (assistant suggested, 2023-05-29)
- Choose wire colors matching actual '69 Camaro engine compartment wires; use reference photos.
- Secure wire bundles to engine/chassis with wire clips and wire ties, routed per reference photos.
