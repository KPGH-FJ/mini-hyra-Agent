### Moab / Utah Destination Knowledge (assistant-provided @2023-04-29)
Attractions:
- Arches National Park in Moab has over 2,000 natural sandstone arches including Delicate Arch, Balanced Rock and Landscape Arch.
- Canyonlands National Park near Moab is divided into Island in the Sky, The Needles, and The Maze districts.
- Dead Horse Point State Park offers views of the Colorado River via a scenic drive and hike.
- Downtown Moab has shops, restaurants, galleries and the Moab Museum.

Hikes:
- Delicate Arch Trail — 3-mile round-trip hike to Delicate Arch.
- Chesler Park Trail (Canyonlands) — 5.5-mile loop through a scenic valley of sandstone formations.
- Fisher Towers Trail — 4.5-mile out-and-back hike to a unique rock formation with Colorado River views.
- Grandstaff Canyon Trail — 3.5-mile out-and-back hike through a scenic canyon with wildlife and waterfalls.

Scenic drives:
- La Sal Mountain Loop Road — recommended scenic drive near Moab.
- Potash Road — recommended scenic drive near Moab.

Best times to visit Moab:
- Spring (March to May): 60s–70s °F, fewer crowds, ideal for off-roading, hiking and biking.
- Fall (September to November): comfortable temperatures, smaller crowds.
- Winter (December to February): 40s–50s °F, some trail closures, fewer crowds, lower prices.
- Summer (June to August): peak season, 90–100 °F, crowds, long lines, higher prices.

Events & festivals:
- Easter Jeep Safari (late March to early April): iconic off-roading event drawing thousands and causing trail closures.
- Moab Off-Road (mid-April): three-day off-road racing event with professional drivers.
- Arches-Canyonlands Science Festival (late April): free event with guided hikes, talks and activities.
- Moab Music Festival (September): classical performances by world-renowned musicians.
- Moab Folk Festival (November): folk music, craft beer and local artisans.
- Moab WinterFest (December): snowshoeing, cross-country skiing and holiday activities.

Day trips from Moab:
- Canyonlands Island in the Sky District — 1 hour 15 minutes away, scenic overlooks and the White Rim Overlook hike.
- Dead Horse Point State Park — 30 minutes away, Colorado River overlooks.
- Hovenweep National Monument — 2 hours away, six ancient Puebloan ruins including Hovenweep House.
- Zion National Park — 4 hours away, the Narrows, Emerald Pools and Temple of Sinawava.
- Bryce Canyon National Park — 4.5 hours away, Queen's Garden Trail and rim scenic drive.
- Mesa Verde National Park (Colorado) — 5.5 hours away, cliff dwellings like Cliff Palace and Balcony House.
- Natural Bridges National Monument — 2.5 hours away, three natural bridges and the Sipapu Bridge Trail.

Canyonlands Island in the Sky / White Rim Road:
- Island in the Sky Scenic Drive is a 12-mile paved road with pull-outs including Grand View Point, Buck Canyon and White Rim Overlooks.
- White Rim Road is a 100-mile dirt road down to the White Rim Plateau, suitable for high-clearance vehicles and requiring a permit.
- White Rim Overlook Trail — 2-mile round-trip hike with Colorado River views, popular for sunrise and sunset.
- Grand View Point Trail — 2-mile round-trip hike to panoramic canyon and mesa views.
- Mesa Arch Trail — 1-mile round-trip hike to an arch perched on a cliff edge.
- Upheaval Dome Trail — 1.7-mile round-trip hike to a formation believed to be a meteorite impact crater.
- A High-Clearance Vehicle Permit is required for all vehicles driving the White Rim Road, obtainable at the Canyonlands Visitor Center or online.
- A Backcountry Permit with a fee is required for overnight camping or backcountry activities on the White Rim Road.
- White Rim Road requires a high-clearance 4x4 vehicle with at least 8–10 inches of ground clearance and tires in good condition.
- Canyonlands limits groups on the White Rim Road to 10 vehicles.
- A typical White Rim Road trip takes 2–3 days depending on pace and activities.
- Suggested White Rim itinerary: Day 1 Island in the Sky to Murphy Hogback Campground, Day 2 to White Crack Campground, Day 3 return to Island in the Sky.
- There are no services or fuel stations along the White Rim Road, so bring enough fuel, food and water.

- Maroon Bells Scenic Trail is a 10-mile out-and-back hike with about 1,500 feet of elevation gain, moderate to challenging.
- Best time to hike is late June to early October when snow has melted and wildflowers bloom.
- Maroon Bells Shuttle Bus runs 8:30 am to 5:00 pm and should be reserved since trailhead parking is limited [suggestion].

Scenic drives / paths:

- Castle Creek Road — 13-mile drive following Castle Creek with mountain views and access to hiking trails.
- Smuggler Mountain Road — 4-mile drive to the top of Smuggler Mountain with panoramic views of Aspen.
- Rio Grande Trail — scenic pedestrian path in Aspen following the Roaring Fork River with mountain views.
